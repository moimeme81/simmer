# Linking Simmers

Run Simmer for yourself and a friend runs his own? Link the two servers and you can still share: recipes, shopping lists, single meals and whole collections, person to person. Each of you keeps your own server, accounts and data.

## Link two servers (admins, once)

Both Simmers need to be reachable by the other, so use their https addresses.

1. **You:** Account → **Linked Simmers** → *Link another Simmer*. Enter a name, your friend's Simmer address, and leave the token empty to make one. Copy the token that appears (it is shown once) and send it to your friend privately.
2. **Your friend (his admin):** the same form, with *your* address and the **same token**.
3. Press **Test** on either side. It works only when both sides agree on the token.

Only admins can link or unlink. Anyone can then share with people on the linked Simmer. Linking means you trust that server's admin: anyone who knows the token can send things to your users, so keep it private. Unlinking removes everything shared across that link on your side.

## Share

Use **Share with another Simmer** (it appears once a link exists) on:
- a recipe page,
- a shopping list (the globe button),
- a meal in the meal plan,
- a collection (open the collection on the Recipes page).

Enter the person's **username on their Simmer**. What they get:

| You share | They get |
| --- | --- |
| Recipe | A read-only copy with its photo that updates when you change it. They can keep their own notes, cook from it, scale it, put it in their plan and lists. |
| Shopping list | The list itself, live both ways: either of you can add, tick and remove items. Changes are applied on the owner's server and sent back at once. |
| Meal | The meal in their meal plan, with its recipe, following your changes. |
| Collection | Every recipe of yours in it. Recipes added later show up, ones you take out disappear. |

Everything is opt-in, one share at a time. **Account → Linked Simmers** lists everything you have shared with its status, and *Stop sharing* removes it on the other side. If your friend deletes something you shared, Simmer remembers that and does not send it again; the share shows "They removed it".

If the other server is down, changes wait and are sent when it is back (it retries every minute).

## Good to know

- Copies belong to the person who shared them, so they cannot be re-shared onward, and they are not included in exports or backups.
- Shopping-list *settings* (categories, stores, name) are changed by the list's owner.
- The photo is sent with the recipe (up to 3.5 MB).
